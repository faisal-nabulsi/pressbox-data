How the score works: Overall /100 is a weighted blend of five components each scored 1–10, normalized so 100 is always the max. Default weights — Prestige ×3.5, Sophomore fit ×2.5, Pay ×1.5, Location ×1.5, Timing ×1 — reflect that brand-name experience compounds, and that a program she can't enter until senior year is worth less right now. Disagree? Drag the sliders under Score weights in the filters and the whole ranking recalculates; your weights are remembered on this device.
